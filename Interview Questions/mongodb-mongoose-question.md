# MongoDB & Mongoose Interview Questions

A collection of MongoDB and Mongoose interview questions covering **database design, documents, schemas, models, CRUD operations, queries, indexes, aggregation, relationships, validation, and database optimization**.

---

# MongoDB & Mongoose Fundamentals

### Topic: Database Design, Schemas & Queries

1. **Q1.** What is MongoDB and how does it differ from SQL databases?

2. **Q2.** What is a document in MongoDB and what format does it use?

3. **Q3.** What is Mongoose and what advantages does it offer over the MongoDB driver?

4. **Q4.** What are Mongoose schemas and models?

5. **Q5.** What is the difference between `find()`, `findOne()`, and `findById()` in Mongoose?

6. **Q6.** How do you perform CRUD operations in Mongoose?

7. **Q7.** What are MongoDB indexes and why are they important?

8. **Q8.** What is the aggregation pipeline in MongoDB?

9. **Q9.** What is population (`populate()`) in Mongoose and when do you use it?

10. **Q10.** What are MongoDB operators like `$set`, `$push`, `$pull`, and `$in`?

11. **Q11.** How do you handle validation in Mongoose schemas?

12. **Q12.** What is the difference between embedding and referencing documents in MongoDB?

13. **Q13.** What is a MongoDB ObjectId and how is it generated?

14. **Q14.** How do you connect a Node.js application to MongoDB using Mongoose?

15. **Q15.** What are Mongoose middleware (`pre` and `post` hooks) and when are they useful?

# MongoDB & Mongoose Advanced

### Topic: Queries, Relationships, Performance & Transactions

16. **Q16.** What is the difference between `updateOne()`, `updateMany()`, and `findOneAndUpdate()`?

17. **Q17.** What is the difference between `deleteOne()`, `deleteMany()`, and `findOneAndDelete()`?

18. **Q18.** What is the difference between `$match`, `$group`, `$sort`, `$project`, and `$lookup` in aggregation?

19. **Q19.** What is the difference between `populate()` and MongoDB's `$lookup`?

20. **Q20.** How do you create a unique index in MongoDB?

21. **Q21.** What is a compound index and when would you use one?

22. **Q22.** What is the difference between `skip()` and cursor-based pagination?

23. **Q23.** How do you implement pagination in MongoDB?

24. **Q24.** What is `.lean()` in Mongoose and when should you use it?

25. **Q25.** What is the difference between `save()` and `create()` in Mongoose?

26. **Q26.** What are MongoDB transactions and when should you use them?

27. **Q27.** What is database normalization and denormalization in MongoDB?

28. **Q28.** How do you optimize slow MongoDB queries?

29. **Q29.** What is the difference between SQL JOINs and MongoDB relationships?

30. **Q30.** How do you design a scalable MongoDB database for a large application?
